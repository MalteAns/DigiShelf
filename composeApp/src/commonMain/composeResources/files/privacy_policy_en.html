2 Nature and Scope of Data Processing

2.1 Google Books API

2.1.1 Which of your data is processed

The following data is sent to the API when needed:

  • Entered or scanned ISBN
  • Entered title
  • Entered author

2.1.2 Description and purpose of data processing

To retrieve book information based on the ISBN, title, and author, the entered data is sent to the Google Books API.

2.1.3 Legal basis for data processing

The processing of your data is based on Art. 6 (1) lit. b GDPR for the performance of the user contract, to provide you with the desired book information.

2.1.4 How long your data is stored

See https://books.google.com/googlebooks/privacy.html

2.1.5 Who receives your data

The data is sent to the Google Books API. See also https://books.google.com/googlebooks/privacy.html

3 Your Rights as a Data Subject

We also point out your further rights as a data subject within the meaning of Art. 13 et seq. GDPR. In particular, you have the right to

  • Access (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • Rectification (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • Erasure (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • Restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • Data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • Objection to processing (Art. 21 GDPR)
  • Withdrawal of consent (Art. 7 (3) GDPR)

Your data is not subject to decisions based solely on automated processing – including profiling (Art. 22 GDPR).

Of course, you can assert your rights with me by sending me an informal message by email to digishelf@malteans.de.

Furthermore, you have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority at any time in accordance with Art. 77 GDPR.

The personal data collected by the App is required if you want the App to fulfill its function. Failure to provide your data will result in the relevant service or function not being able to be provided.
